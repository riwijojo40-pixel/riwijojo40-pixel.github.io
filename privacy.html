Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how yt-uploader (“the Application”) accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data when you authorize it to manage your YouTube channels.

1. Who we are

yt-uploader is a personal tool operated by its owner to upload and manage videos on YouTube channels the owner controls. It is not a multi-tenant commercial service.

2. Google / YouTube data we access

With your explicit OAuth consent, the Application may access YouTube Data API data such as:

  • Your YouTube channel identity and basic channel metadata
  • Video upload, update, and deletion on channels you authorize
  • Playlist listing and adding videos to playlists you select
  • Thumbnail and video metadata needed to complete uploads

The Application only requests the OAuth scopes required for these features.

3. How we use the data

Google user data is used solely to:

  • Authenticate you to YouTube
  • Upload, update, or delete videos on your authorized channels
  • Add videos to playlists and apply your saved default upload settings
  • Show upload status and basic channel/video information inside the Application

We do not use Google user data for advertising, analytics products sold to others, or training AI models. Use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How we store the data

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner’s computer (or private server) so the Application can call YouTube APIs on your behalf.
  • Upload queue items, channel settings, and related operational logs are stored locally with the Application.
  • Video and thumbnail files you choose to upload remain under your control on your storage until uploaded to YouTube.

5. Sharing

We do not sell Google user data. We do not share Google user data with third parties, except as needed to operate the YouTube / Google APIs you authorized (i.e., sending API requests to Google).

6. Retention and deletion

You can revoke the Application’s access at any time in your Google Account permissions. After revocation, stored OAuth tokens should be deleted from the Application, and it will no longer access your YouTube data. You may also ask the operator to delete locally stored tokens and related records.

7. Security

Tokens and credentials are kept in local application storage with restricted file permissions where practical. Protect access to the computer or network where the Application runs.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about yt-uploader, contact the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen for this application.

9. Changes

If how we handle Google user data changes, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date revised.
