yt-uploader

Personal YouTube upload helper for channel operators

yt-uploader is a private web tool that helps the owner upload videos to their own YouTube channels, manage playlists, and maintain channel default settings. It runs on the owner’s own computer and talks to YouTube only through the official YouTube Data API with OAuth 2.0.

What it does

  • Upload videos and thumbnails to the owner’s YouTube channels
  • Add uploaded videos to selected playlists
  • Read basic channel and video metadata needed for uploads
  • Optionally delete or update videos the owner uploaded

Who it is for

This application is used only by its owner to operate their own YouTube channels. It is not offered as a public product to third-party end users.

Privacy

How Google / YouTube user data is accessed, used, and stored is described in our Privacy Policy.
